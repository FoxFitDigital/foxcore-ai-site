NordVPN vs PureVPN 2026: Which VPN Is Better for UK Users?

📅 Updated May 2026 ⌛ 8 min read 🇬🇧 UK-focused

Quick Verdict

NordVPN wins for most UK users. It is faster, has more UK servers, and its no-log policy has been independently audited multiple times. PureVPN is a solid alternative if you want the lowest possible price on a long-term plan — but on overall value and reliability, NordVPN leads.

The Short Version

Both NordVPN and PureVPN are established VPN providers that work well for UK users. The question is which one is worth paying for. We looked at pricing, UK server coverage, speed, security credentials, and ease of use to give you a clear answer.

If you want the summary: NordVPN is the better all-round VPN. PureVPN is cheaper but trades off some features and server breadth to get there.

Pricing

PureVPN is cheaper, particularly on its longer-term plans. At time of writing, its 2-year plan comes in under £2/month, while NordVPN's equivalent is typically around £2.99 to £3.49/month depending on the current promotion.

Both VPNs run frequent discount offers, so the gap between them narrows during sales. On standard pricing, NordVPN's extra cost buys you more server coverage, more advanced features (Threat Protection, Onion over VPN), and a stronger no-log audit trail.

If budget is the primary concern and you do not need streaming unblocking beyond the basics, PureVPN is a reasonable choice. For most users, the £1/month premium for NordVPN is worth it.

UK Server Coverage

NordVPN has over 600 servers in the UK, spread across London, Manchester and other locations. This density matters: more servers means less congestion, faster connections during peak hours, and more stable streaming performance.

PureVPN has a smaller UK server footprint. It covers the essentials and speeds are generally acceptable, but during peak times UK performance can be less consistent than NordVPN.

Speed

On WireGuard (the fastest VPN protocol), NordVPN consistently tops independent speed tests. Average download speeds in the UK typically remain above 80% of base connection speed, which is excellent for streaming, gaming, or working remotely.

PureVPN's WireGuard implementation is also quick — it has improved significantly since 2023 — but NordVPN maintains a reliable edge in most independent benchmarks.

Security and Privacy

Both providers use AES-256 encryption and support modern protocols including WireGuard and OpenVPN. Neither logs user activity.

NordVPN has the stronger privacy credentials: it has been audited multiple times by Big Four firms (PwC and Deloitte) with clean reports. It also offers Onion over VPN routing and a built-in Threat Protection feature that blocks ads, malware links, and trackers without needing a separate browser extension.

PureVPN was audited by KPMG in 2023, confirming its no-log status. That is a solid credential, though the audit history is thinner than NordVPN's.

Streaming Performance

Both VPNs unblock BBC iPlayer, ITV Hub, Channel 4 and Disney+. NordVPN is more reliable at maintaining access after streaming services update their VPN detection — it is faster to roll out fixes when blocks occur.

For Netflix, NordVPN's dedicated streaming servers (P2P and Obfuscated) give it an edge. PureVPN works with Netflix but requires switching servers manually if the first one is blocked.

Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose NordVPN if: you want the fastest UK performance, best streaming reliability, built-in ad blocking, or the strongest independent privacy audit trail.

Choose PureVPN if: you want the lowest price on a long-term plan and do not need Threat Protection or Onion over VPN. It is a solid, reliable VPN for everyday browsing and is particularly good value on its 2 or 3-year plans.
